# Decision Log

## Decision Format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision Title

#### Decision
What was decided.

#### Reason
Why that decision was made.

#### Alternatives
What was considered.

#### Safety Impact
Low / Medium / High.

#### Status
Accepted / Rejected / Superseded.
